# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Program Manager (TPM)

### Role Summary
Technical Program Managers coordinate large cross-team technical initiatives, manage dependencies, and ensure engineering trade-offs and timelines are visible to leadership and stakeholders. They bridge technical and organizational realities.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate and sequence work across multiple engineering teams
- Identify, track, and mitigate cross-team technical dependencies
- Maintain program-level timelines and milestone maps
- Make engineering trade-offs visible to Product Managers and sponsors
- Escalate and resolve technical blockers that span team boundaries
- Facilitate architecture and design alignment across teams

### Goals
- Deliver large initiatives on time by removing cross-team friction
- Reduce rework and delays caused by dependency misalignment
- Enable informed decision-making about technical trade-offs and resource allocation

### Typical Communication
- Weekly cross-team sync meetings
- Dependency maps and critical path updates
- Executive summaries of technical decisions and risks
- Escalation reports to Product Lead and Sponsors

---

## Release / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
Release and Platform Engineers own the infrastructure, automation, and processes that enable safe, reliable deployments. They ensure CI/CD pipelines are robust, deployments are repeatable, and rollback procedures are tested.

### Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Own release procedures, deployment windows, and rollback plans
- Implement and monitor post-deployment verification and smoke tests
- Ensure security scanning and compliance gates are in place
- Document deployment runbooks and incident response procedures
- Coordinate with developers on deployability and observability

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize deployment-related incidents and rollbacks
- Reduce time to detect and recover from production issues

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with Project Managers
- Deployment checklists and runbooks
- Post-deploy verification reports
- On-call incident response coordination

---

## UX Researcher / Designer

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Designers lead user research, design solutions, validate usability, and provide design specifications and acceptance criteria. They ensure features meet user needs and provide excellent experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and validation studies
- Design solutions and create design specifications
- Validate usability and accessibility of features
- Provide design acceptance criteria and component specs
- Conduct design reviews and iterate based on feedback
- Collaborate with Product Managers and Engineers on feasibility

### Goals
- Ensure features solve real user problems
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce rework by catching usability issues early

### Typical Communication
- Design specs and prototypes during planning
- Design review sessions with engineering and product
- User research findings and recommendations
- Acceptance criteria and design system updates

---

## Data Analyst / Analytics Owner

### Role Summary
Data Analysts and Analytics Owners define success metrics, instrument tracking, analyze experiments and releases, and create dashboards that inform product and business decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and key performance indicators (KPIs) during planning
- Instrument features with analytics tracking and logging
- Analyze experiment results and release impact
- Build and maintain performance dashboards
- Provide data-driven insights to Product Managers and leadership
- Identify anomalies and performance regressions

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making across the organization
- Measure and demonstrate business impact of features
- Detect issues early through proactive monitoring

### Typical Communication
- Metrics definitions during project planning
- Release impact reports and analysis
- Dashboard updates and performance reviews
- Recommendations based on data insights

---

## Security & Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads assess security and privacy risks, review designs and implementations for compliance, and coordinate security scanning and approvals. They ensure projects meet organizational security standards and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security and privacy risks during planning and design
- Review feature designs and implementations for security and compliance
- Coordinate security scanning and penetration testing
- Gate releases when security reviews are required
- Maintain and communicate security policies and best practices
- Investigate and respond to security incidents and vulnerabilities

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data leaks
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements
- Foster a security-conscious development culture

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews during planning
- Compliance and risk assessments
- Security incident alerts and response coordination
- Security policy updates and training materials

---

## Product Operations (ProdOps) / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Product Operations and Support Liaisons manage release communications, support runbooks, enablement materials, and post-release monitoring. They ensure stakeholders and support teams are prepared and equipped for feature launches.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain support runbooks and troubleshooting guides
- Create enablement materials and training for support and sales teams
- Manage release communications to customers and stakeholders
- Monitor customer support channels for issues post-release
- Coordinate with support teams on customer feedback and escalations
- Track and report on adoption and customer feedback

### Goals
- Ensure smooth customer adoption of new features
- Reduce support volume and escalations through clear documentation
- Capture customer feedback to inform future improvements

### Typical Communication
- Release announcement drafts and customer communications
- Support runbooks and enablement guides
- Post-release support metrics and customer feedback summaries
- Customer success and adoption reports
